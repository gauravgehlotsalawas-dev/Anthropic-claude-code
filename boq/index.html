e-Tender BOQ for NIC / GePNIC portals

Writes this project's BOQ into the department's own BOQ template (.xls) from the e-procurement portal (eproc.rajasthan.gov.in and other GePNIC portals): Percentage, Item Rate or Item Wise BoQ, versions 3.0 and 4.0. The template's macros, locked cells, drop-downs and formulas are kept, so the file goes through the portal's BOQ validation like a hand-made one.

  1. Add the BOQ template downloaded from the portal
  2. Fill in the tender details and choose the items
  3. Download, open in MS Excel with macros enabled, Save, and upload
